## System map (the money loop)

```
Audience → Trust content → Clear CTA → Tracked affiliate link
    → Signup / purchase → Commission → Feedback into next asset
```

**Trust content** = honest demos, comparisons, workflows, and "I use this because…" stories.  
**Clear CTA** = one primary action per asset.  
**Tracked link** = you can attribute revenue to a specific post/email/video.

## SOP 1 — Offer selection (20 minutes)

Score each offer 1–5 on:

1. **Audience fit** — Would my people actually need this?  
2. **Proof** — Can I demo it honestly this week?  
3. **Economics** — Payout × realistic conversion (not fantasy EPC).  
4. **Cookie & attribution** — Length, last-click rules, coupon requirements.  
5. **Partner quality** — Creative assets, support, brand reputation, refund rate.  
6. **Compliance risk** — Health/finance claims? Restricted niches?  

**Rule:** Only promote what you would recommend without a commission. If the answer is "no," skip it.

**Decision:** Join the highest-scoring offer you can ship content for this week. Park the rest.

## SOP 2 — Content engine (weekly batch)

**Monday (45–60 min)**  
- Pick one angle: comparison, workflow, teardown, or "mistakes I made."  
- Run Research Prompt → outline → draft with Prompt Pack.  

**Tuesday (30 min)**  
- Edit for voice. Add screenshots or a short Loom. Insert tracked CTA + disclosure.  

**Wednesday (20 min)**  
- Ship to primary channel. Cross-post a shortened version elsewhere.  

**Thursday (30 min)**  
- Reply to comments/DMs. Capture objections → turn into FAQ assets.  

**Friday (20 min)**  
- Log metrics. Note one improvement for next week.

**Batch tip:** Write 3 variants of the CTA and subject line once; rotate them.

## SOP 3 — Tracking hygiene

Maintain a simple sheet with columns:

| Date | Asset title | Channel | Offer | Link/UTM | Clicks | Signups | Revenue | Notes |
|------|-------------|---------|-------|----------|--------|---------|---------|-------|

**Rules**

- One UTM campaign per major asset (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`).  
- Prefer program deep links over homepage links when possible.  
- Never reuse a "mystery" link across unrelated posts.  
- Reconcile dashboard vs. sheet monthly (payouts lag).

## Prompt pack (copy/paste)

### P1 — Niche & offer research

```
You are an affiliate strategist for a solo creator.
Niche: {NICHE}
Audience: {WHO} who care about {OUTCOME}.
I already use or can demo: {TOOLS}.

1) List 8 affiliate-friendly product categories that match this audience.
2) For each, propose 1–2 specific products and why they'd convert.
3) Flag compliance-sensitive categories (health, finance, kids, etc.).
4) Recommend my top 3 to pursue this month with a 1-sentence rationale each.
Be concrete. No fluff.
```

### P2 — Comparison page draft

```
Write a fair, creator-voice comparison of {PRODUCT_A} vs {PRODUCT_B} for {AUDIENCE}.
Structure:
- Who each is for
- Setup time & learning curve
- Pricing (public info only; note "check site for current pricing")
- Pros/cons (honest)
- When I'd pick A vs B
- CTA with placeholder AFFILIATE_LINK_A / AFFILIATE_LINK_B
- Short disclosure paragraph at top and near CTA
Tone: direct, practical, zero hype. 900–1,200 words.
```

### P3 — Workflow email (newsletter)

```
Write a newsletter email teaching one workflow using {PRODUCT}.
Audience: {AUDIENCE}. Goal: one clear CTA to try {PRODUCT} via {AFFILIATE_LINK}.
Include:
- Subject + preview text (3 options)
- Story hook (problem → attempt → fix)
- 5–7 step workflow
- Soft CTA mid-email, primary CTA at end
- FTC-style disclosure near the CTA
Length: ~500–700 words. Conversational.
```

### P4 — Short-form / social thread

```
Turn this long-form angle into a 8–12 post thread for {PLATFORM}:
Angle: {ANGLE}
Offer: {PRODUCT}
Rules: one idea per post, concrete tips, final post = CTA + disclosure.
No engagement bait. No false urgency.
```

### P5 — Objection FAQ

```
From these comments/DMs: {PASTE}
Generate:
1) Top 8 objections clustered
2) Honest answers (acknowledge limits)
3) When to recommend an alternative instead of {PRODUCT}
4) 3 FAQ blocks I can paste under a landing/comparison page
```

### P6 — A/B CTA variants

```
Product: {PRODUCT}. Benefit: {PRIMARY_BENEFIT}. Link: {AFFILIATE_LINK}
Write 10 CTA lines (button + surrounding sentence) for:
- Comparison page
- Email
- YouTube description
Vary specificity and risk-reversal. Keep disclosures separate (don't hide them in the CTA).
```

## FTC disclosure checklist (US-focused)

Use this before every publish. Adapt for your jurisdiction if outside the US.

**Before publish**

- [ ] Material connection exists (commission, free product, bonus, or paid relationship)  
- [ ] Disclosure is **clear and conspicuous** (not buried in bio-only or "#ad" in a wall of tags)  
- [ ] Disclosure appears **near the endorsement / CTA**, not only at the end if the pitch is early  
- [ ] Language is plain: e.g. "I may earn a commission if you buy through my links—at no extra cost to you."  
- [ ] Platform-specific rules checked (YouTube paid promotion toggle, Instagram branded content, etc.)  
- [ ] No misleading claims: results, income, health, or guarantees you can't support  
- [ ] Screenshots/demos match current product behavior  
- [ ] Affiliate links work and go where you say they go  

**Good short disclosures (examples)**

- "Affiliate disclosure: I may earn a commission if you purchase through links in this post."  
- "This email contains affiliate links. I only recommend tools I use or would use."  

**Avoid**

- Hiding links without saying they're affiliate  
- Fake "independent" reviews when you were paid or given free product without disclosure  
- Income claims without context and evidence  

*This checklist is educational, not legal advice. When in doubt, over-disclose and consult a professional for regulated niches.*
