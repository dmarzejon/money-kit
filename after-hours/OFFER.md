# Offer: After-Hours Lead Desk for Home Service Businesses

**Niche (locked):** HVAC · Plumbing · Electrical · Roofing · Cleaning (owner-operated, 2–25 trucks)  
**Positioning:** Productized AI automation — not custom consulting  
**Promise:** Never miss another after-hours or callback lead. First response in under 60 seconds. Booked jobs on your calendar.

---

## The product

### Name
**After-Hours Lead Desk** — AI receptionist + speed-to-lead follow-up for home service companies.

### Who it's for
Owners who:
- Get 5–40 inbound leads/week (Google, Angi, Yelp, Facebook, website forms)
- Miss calls nights/weekends or drown in voicemails
- Lose jobs to competitors who text back first
- Will not hire a full-time CSR ($45k+) but will pay for a system that books jobs

### Who it's not for
- Solo operators with <5 leads/month (ROI too thin)
- Enterprise franchises with existing call centers
- Anyone wanting "build me a custom GPT" with no ops process

## Deliverables (what they actually get)

### Setup (all packages)
1. **Intake & call-flow map** (1 call, 45 min) — hours, service area, job types, pricing bands, disqualify rules
2. **AI voice + SMS receptionist** — answers missed/after-hours calls or overflows; qualifies; offers bookable slots
3. **Web form → instant SMS/email** — sub-60s response with booking link or callback window
4. **Calendar booking** — Google Calendar / Jobber / ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro (or email fallback)
5. **Owner alert** — SMS/email when a hot job is booked or needs human close
6. **Go-live checklist + 14-day tuning** — script tweaks from real transcripts

### Monthly retainer
- Uptime monitoring & provider failovers
- Script/prompt updates (seasonal: AC rush, freeze bursts, storm roofing)
- Weekly lead → booked → no-show report (Growth+)
- 1 async optimization pass per month (Starter) / biweekly (Growth) / weekly (Fleet)
- Priority support via shared Slack/email channel

### Explicitly NOT included
Custom mobile apps · full website redesign · Google Ads management · CSR hiring · legal/compliance opinions

## Objection handlers

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have an answering service." | Answering services take messages. We **qualify and book**. Message pads don't put jobs on the calendar at 11pm. |
| "AI will sound robotic / piss off customers." | We use your trade language, local area names, and a human handoff for angry/complex calls. You approve the script before go-live. We'll play you a sample on the demo call. |
| "Too expensive." | Half a CSR. One recovered job often covers a month. Starter is $497/mo after setup — cheaper than missing two weekend calls. |
| "We use ServiceTitan / Jobber already." | Great — we plug into it. This sits **in front** of your CRM, not instead of it. |
| "Can you just build a chatbot?" | Chatbots without call + SMS + booking + follow-up don't move revenue. We sell booked jobs, not widgets. |
| "Need to think about it." | Fair. I'll send the one-pager and a 3-minute demo clip. If you're still getting after-hours voicemails next week, the cost of waiting is measurable. Soft CTA: 20-min calendar link. |
| "What if it books junk leads?" | Disqualify rules: outside service area, no address, tire-kickers asking only for ballpark with no phone. You set the filters; we tune weekly. |
