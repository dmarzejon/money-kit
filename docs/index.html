FAQ

Will it sound like a robot?

We write scripts in your trade language with local area cues. You approve before go-live. Complex or upset callers get a human handoff path.

We already pay an answering service.

They take messages. We qualify and book. A notepad at 11pm doesn’t put a water-heater job on tomorrow’s route.

Does this replace Jobber / ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro?

No. It sits in front of your stack and writes bookings into it (or Google Calendar as a clean start).

How fast to go live?

Typical: kickoff call, access checklist, script approval, then live in about 10–14 days — faster if your calendar and numbers are ready.

Who is this not for?

Under ~5 leads/month, or teams that already run a full call center. We’re built for owner-operated home service companies that lose work after hours.
