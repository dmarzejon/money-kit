# 5 AI affiliate workflows you can run this week

**Free 1-pager from the Operator Money Kit** · [Full kit — $29 waitlist](mailto:toolsthathelp@agentmail.to?subject=Operator%20Money%20Kit%20waitlist)

You don't need a funnel empire. You need five repeatable workflows, honest disclosure, and tracked links. Run one per weekday.

---

## 1) Offer scorecard (Mon · 20 min)

**Goal:** Pick one product worth promoting.

Paste this into your AI assistant with your niche filled in:

> Score these affiliate offers 1–5 on audience fit, demo-ability this week, payout realism, cookie length, and compliance risk. Recommend one to ship content for by Friday. Offers: {LIST}. Niche: {NICHE}.

**Done when:** You're enrolled (or applied) to one program and have a tracked link.

---

## 2) Comparison draft (Tue · 45 min)

**Goal:** One fair A vs B page or newsletter.

Prompt:

> Write a fair comparison of {A} vs {B} for {AUDIENCE}. Include who each is for, setup time, honest pros/cons, when you'd pick each, CTAs with link placeholders, and a clear affiliate disclosure at top and near CTA. No hype. ~1,000 words.

**Done when:** Draft edited in your voice + disclosure + working links.

---

## 3) Workflow email (Wed · 30 min)

**Goal:** Teach one job-to-be-done, soft-sell the tool.

Prompt:

> Newsletter teaching a 5–7 step workflow using {PRODUCT}. Subject + preview (3 options), story hook, steps, mid + end CTA, disclosure near CTA. ~600 words.

**Done when:** Scheduled or sent with UTM'd link.

---

## 4) Objection → FAQ (Thu · 25 min)

**Goal:** Turn real pushback into trust assets.

Prompt:

> From these comments: {PASTE}. Cluster objections, write honest answers, note when to recommend an alternative, output 3 FAQ blocks for my page.

**Done when:** FAQs pasted under your comparison or pinned in community.

---

## 5) Metrics Friday (Fri · 15 min)

**Goal:** Learn, don't guess.

Log for each asset: clicks, signups, revenue (even $0), and one note ("CTA weak" / "wrong audience" / "keep").

**Rule:** Kill or rewrite anything with attention but zero intent after a fair sample. Double down on formats that get signups.

---

## Compliance in one line

If you earn a commission (or got free product), **say so clearly near the pitch**—every time.
