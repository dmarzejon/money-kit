# Playbook: X Original Content Rewards (OCR)

**Context (as of Sep 11, 2026):** Creator Revenue Sharing retired ~Sep 7, 2026. Replaced by **Original Content Rewards** — pays on qualified impressions of *original* content from Premium/verified users. Reply farms and pure reposts are the wrong game.

---

## Qualify (checklist for Diego)

| Requirement | Status action |
|-------------|---------------|
| X Premium (or Premium+) | Diego: subscribe / confirm |
| ≥500 verified followers | Grow via original posts + selective follows in AI/SMB niches; no bots |
| ≥500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in 90 days | Volume of *original* posts that get real distribution |
| Original content focus | Write analysis, frameworks, demos — not quote-retweet spam |
| AI disclosure | Label AI-generated **video**/media per X rules; text drafts may be AI-assisted but must be edited into your voice — never pass off stolen/reposted media as original |
| Payout hygiene | Biweekly; ~$30 minimum threshold — keep payment method ready when eligible |

**Agent vs Diego split**
- **Agent:** draft posts, calendar, thread research, track impressions weekly once API/analytics access exists  
- **Diego:** Premium, final post button (until X connected), account safety decisions, payment setup

## Disclosure rules (AI)

- **AI video / synthetic media:** disclose clearly in the post (X can suspend OCR access ~90 days for undisclosed AI video).  
- **AI-assisted text:** rewrite so it’s your take; add a real opinion, local number, or screenshot from *our* lab.  
- **Never:** scrape someone else’s thread and repost as new; never use copyrighted clips as “original.”  
- **When unsure:** add “Drafted with AI assist; numbers from our lab / public OCR rules” in the thread’s last reply.

## Growth tactics that stay OCR-safe

- Quote *with new analysis* (your chart, your framework) — not empty quote RTs  
- End posts with a sharp question to invite *quality* replies  
- Pin the best evergreen OCR or offer thread  
- Soft CTA max 2×/week to landing (After-Hours Lead Desk)  
- No giveaway-for-follow spam; no purchased followers
